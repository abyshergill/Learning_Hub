## Chapter 2: The Recipe vs. The Cake (Images vs. Containers)

Before you can play with your magic boxes, you need to understand the two most important words in Docker: **Images** and **Containers**.

Let's look at the difference:

| Concept | What it is in Docker | The Real-World Analogy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Docker Image** | A frozen template of your app. It cannot change. | **The Recipe Book** (or a cookie cutter). |
| **Docker Container** | The live, running version of your app. | **The Actual Cake** (or the real cookie you eat). |

If you want 50 matching cookies, you don't recreate them from scratch every time; you just stomp your cookie cutter (Image) into the dough 50 times to get 50 cookies (Containers).
