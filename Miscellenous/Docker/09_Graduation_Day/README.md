
## Chapter 10: Graduation Day! 🎓

Look how far you've come! You started with a basic lunch box analogy, and now you are building real, interconnected web systems.

Here is your official **Docker Captain's Cheat Sheet** to remember your magic spells:

> 📜 **The Captain's Cheat Sheet:**
> * `docker run` — Bakes a recipe into a live, running container box.
> * `docker ps` — Pokes your head in the room to see what boxes are awake.
> * `docker stop` — Tells a running box it's time to take a nap.
> * `docker rm` — Throws away an old box so your room stays clean.
> * `-p` — Builds a secret hallway (Port) to connect your computer to the box.
> * `-v` — Cuts a magic window (Volume) to share folders with the box.

You have officially unlocked the core secrets of Docker!
